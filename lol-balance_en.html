Analytical Balance Assistant (League of Legends)

MBA Capstone Project - Ibmec BH

The Objective

This project demonstrates the application of data science to solve a critical issue in the gaming industry: competitive imbalance[cite: 89]. The main goal was to develop an analytical tool using public data to automatically diagnose balance issues and provide actionable insights for developers[cite: 90].

Methodology & Data Collection

To ensure relevance, data was collected via the Riot API, focusing exclusively on matches from the highest competitive tiers (Challenger, Grandmaster, and Master) on the Korean (KR) server[cite: 100, 102]. The process captured comprehensive match details, including champion picks, built items, and duration[cite: 105].

Modeling & Results

A predictive model using the Random Forest algorithm was developed, achieving an 0.87 accuracy rate in predicting match outcomes (win or loss)[cite: 119, 124]. Feature importance metrics revealed crucial insights:

  • Item Impact: The analysis showed that specific items (like Ionian Boots of Lucidity and Plated Steelcaps) have a higher impact on winning than individual champions or runes[cite: 129, 130].
  • Synergies: The model identified that balancing should not focus solely on the champion, but on how they interact with key runes and game mechanics[cite: 131, 132].

This tool acts as a "balance assistant" that goes beyond superficial analysis, diagnosing root causes and enabling fast, evidence-based decision-making to maintain the integrity of the competitive ecosystem[cite: 97, 134].
