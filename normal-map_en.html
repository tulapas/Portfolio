Automated Normal Mapping for 2D Games (Unity Plugin)

Master's Research - Universidade de Aveiro (Team Hó, Hô & Xo)

Overview & Validation

Co-authored an academic paper accepted and presented on stage at Eurographics 2026. Developed alongside Denis Borodin and Lucas Nascimento [cite: 196, 197], this project introduces a custom Unity plugin that solves the production bottleneck in manual normal map creation[cite: 205, 206]. The tool dynamically extracts and generates maps from the grayscale values of 2D pixel art.

Performance & Efficiency Data

We conducted rigorous empirical tests with professional artists, proving the tool's superiority:

  • Productivity Leap: The average manual production time dropped from 19 minutes to just 1 second per asset using the plugin[cite: 218, 219]. This represents an efficiency increase of 1,140x[cite: 220].
  • Aesthetic Quality: In blind visual perception tests, participants unanimously preferred the plugin-generated results, highlighting realistic depth and smoother light/shadow transitions[cite: 242, 252].
  • Professional Approval: The tool achieved a 100% artist recommendation rate for professional workflows, with an average usability score of 9.6/10[cite: 245, 248, 282].

Practical Impact: "Other Half" Project

As Project Manager, Game Designer, and Pixel Artist for the game Other Half, the integration of this technology was a strategic necessity[cite: 42, 44]. Keeping base assets "low-detail" and relying on the plugin for dynamic lighting allowed me to reclaim hours of intense manual labor[cite: 48, 49]. I redirected that time toward refining the level design and expanding our asset library, resulting in a significantly more immersive and cohesive game world[cite: 50, 55].
